# Role language guidance

Scholar Scout uses role-specific language that preserves choice and does not imply an admission, eligibility, or institutional outcome.

- **Students:** explore pathways, compare options, and choose what to verify next. A profile tailors discovery; it is not an admissions decision.
- **Advisor:** offers bounded guidance and recovery steps, never an outcome or a substitute for official sources or qualified support.
- **Institutions:** are described through source-linked facts. Students confirm requirements, costs, deadlines, and policies directly with the institution.
- **Authorized staff:** maintain governed catalogue information and moderation decisions. Client wording never grants access or identifies protected people.

Recovery text states what can be retried or checked without exposing provider, account, reporter, author, contact, or student-private details. Use visible labels, associated helper text, and live status/alert regions for task feedback.
