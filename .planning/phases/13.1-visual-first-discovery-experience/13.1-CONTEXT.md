# Phase 13.1: Visual-First Discovery Experience - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-27
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Make catalogue discovery visually compelling by default without weakening student agency, factual clarity, media-rights safeguards, or motion accessibility. Students should encounter a calm visual arrival when discovery begins and a restrained visual atmosphere across catalogue browse surfaces, while governed facts, source links, comparison, and official next actions remain clear and separate. This phase extends the Phase 13 safe-media boundary; it does not add user uploads, remote aggregation, third-party embeds, personal inference, or a new ranking signal.

</domain>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Visual-first discovery arrival
- **D-01:** Discovery opens with an **arrival panel**: a short visual welcome introduces a possible place, then hands the student to clean, source-first programme cards. Do not place the factual opportunity card, source state, comparison, or official action directly over the visual. — **Reversibility:** costly — this is the first-arrival composition across catalogue discovery and its responsive/UAT expectations.
- **D-02:** The visual layer appears automatically once discovery begins; it is not an optional visual-explorer destination. It must not require a sign-in, a completed student story, passive behavioural profiling, inferred attributes, or a change to catalogue ordering.

### Honest visual fallback
- **D-03:** When an opportunity has no approved visual, use a clearly labelled **Scholar Scout campus illustration** to preserve visual pull. The label must plainly state that it is a Scholar Scout illustration and not an official photograph of that provider campus.
- **D-04:** Preserve the previously locked fallback order: (1) approved local/provider/Scholar Scout visual; (2) Maps or Street View only after separate authorization; (3) visibly labelled Scholar Scout-generated illustration; (4) factual source-first fallback. Do not invent provider affiliation/evidence, use a third-party media path or embed, or treat copied Street View imagery as permission-free.
- **D-05:** Use local reviewed fixtures or safe illustrative fallback states so the visual-first journey is demonstrable during normal local UAT. A missing approved provider visual must still yield an honest, useful, visually intentional state.

### Calm catalogue-wide atmosphere
- **D-06:** Apply a restrained visual atmosphere across catalogue discovery pages rather than only the arrival panel and provider detail. It may use low-contrast, CSS-only depth behind content but never background video or media autoplay. — **Reversibility:** costly — shared discovery shells, detail surfaces, responsive accessibility, and visual regression coverage depend on this presentation contract.
- **D-07:** Respect reduced-motion preferences by rendering the catalogue atmosphere static and disabling the existing narrow media-loop exception. The only allowed looping media remains the previously approved centered card: muted, pauseable, scroll/center bounded, and never active under reduced motion.

### the agent's Discretion
- Choose the exact visual motifs, copy, local illustrative assets, responsive layout, and CSS intensity, provided they keep text/action contrast high, stay visually calm, use no background media, and remain source-first.
- Reuse the governed public snapshot, renderable-media resolver, factual fallback, source/action, semantic controls, focus treatment, and motion-safe visual primitives instead of creating an alternate discovery data path.

### Folded Todos
- **Make discovery visual-first** — the existing optional explorer disappears when a safe fixture is absent, leaving discovery flat. This phase makes visual arrival and calm catalogue atmosphere core while retaining the Phase 13 rights, fallback, and motion constraints.

</decisions>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `CatalogueDiscoveryOverview`, `CatalogueFocusView`, and `DiscoveryPreviewSlot` already separate governed programme facts from display-only media and expose the bounded playback seam.
- `resolveRenderableMedia` projects only approved local assets or labelled illustrations into the reviewed public snapshot and reports a factual fallback otherwise.
- `StudentJourneyScene` and the global styles establish the bright red, white, silver, Space Grotesk, motion-safe visual language to extend.

### Established Patterns
- Learner surfaces read only the reviewed snapshot, retain source/date/status plus a direct verification action, and never request provider content at display time.
- Unknown, expired, revoked, malformed, unsupported, or remote media fails closed to factual copy.
- Native labelled controls, visible focus, responsive reflow, and reduced-motion behaviour are mandatory learner-surface patterns.

### Integration Points
- Evolve the catalogue overview and focus/detail presentation to treat the visual arrival as core, not optional.
- Extend the existing local renderable-media and fallback seams; do not introduce an upload, embed, scraping, or new persistence boundary.
- Add assertions for automatic visual arrival, label honesty, reduced-motion/static ambient behaviour, all-visible factual actions, and local UAT fixtures.

</code_context>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- The visual first impression should feel like a practical realtor-style first look: it catches attention, then lets a student inspect facts and choices more deeply.
- Visuals should help students imagine a future space across regions such as Buffalo, Houston, and Chicago without claiming a generated scene is a verified campus photo.
- The catalogue should feel alive through subtle visual depth, not through a distracting media feed.

</specifics>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- Recruiting students to upload campus or peer content needs separate consent, age/privacy, moderation, rights, takedown, attribution, and retention work.
- Individual student testimonials, outcome stories, and provider-linked personal narratives remain out of scope until their consent, factual, and non-affiliation rules are separately approved.
- A real Google Maps/Street View or another third-party renderer needs separate platform-terms, privacy, accessibility, and source-rights approval; screenshot copying is not approved.
- A visual system that changes the whole application outside catalogue discovery is a later design phase; this phase is intentionally bounded to the catalogue journey.

</deferred>
