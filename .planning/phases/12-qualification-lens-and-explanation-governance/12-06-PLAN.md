<interfaces>
`resolveStudentActor` owns server-derived account identity. Account actors currently expose `storageKey` in the `account:<id>` namespace, and the qualifications route already reads and writes via that property. The learner pages have a NextAuth session but must use the same account-key helper rather than treating the session id as a persistence key.

`getQualificationRecord` receives an internal storage key and returns either one bounded private record or null. `buildQualificationLensModel` accepts only the record's structured choices and explicit keywords; it must never receive the note, onboarding state, proxy data, or a candidate record.

`QualificationRecordForm` currently invokes its close callback after a successful POST. Extend its callback contract so a parent can refresh server-rendered qualification data only after a successful save or clear; preserve conflict and failed-save drafts without refreshing or closing.
</interfaces>
