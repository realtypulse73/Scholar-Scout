# Phase 13.2: Staff Media Intake, Permission Review, and Houston Tillotson Pilot — Specification

**Created:** 2026-09-29  
**Ambiguity score:** 0.10 (gate: ≤ 0.20)  
**Requirements:** 7 locked

## Goal

An invited, signed-in adult student can privately submit one short original video and poster fallback with an explicit rights attestation, while staff alone review, publish, or remove it; Houston Tillotson University is the first non-endorsed, draft-only pilot.

## Background

The governed catalogue already supports staff-only JSON candidate staging, checklist-based review, snapshot publication, and a narrow public media projection. It has no binary upload path, adult-contributor invitation boundary, signer record, private rights evidence store, contributor revocation route, or direct-student media UI. Existing learner-facing media candidates expose public-facing source fields, so private consent evidence must not be placed in that model. The product also has no approved basis to use Google Maps, scraped media, or a real-campus likeness as temporary visual material.

## Requirements

1. **Invite-only adult submission**: Only a staff-invited, authenticated account may open or submit the contributor flow; the account must affirm that the signer is at least 18.
   - Current: Catalogue intake is staff-only and no student account can submit media or a contributor attestation.
   - Target: A bounded adult-contributor invitation state gates the private submission route and UI; unauthenticated, uninvited, disabled, and under-18-declaring accounts have no submission capability.
   - Acceptance: Route and UI tests prove that only an invited authenticated account that affirms 18+ can create a private draft, while all other states receive no asset or consent data.

2. **Contributor rights attestation**: A valid adult submission records a dated, private declaration that the contributor has creator authority and every recognisable person has agreed to the requested Scholar Scout use.
   - Current: `CatalogueMediaRights` captures only a public-facing rights basis and source URL; there is no signer declaration or private evidence boundary.
   - Target: The submission requires nonblank attestation, signer name, consent timestamp, and a bounded rights basis before acceptance; this record is visible only to authorized reviewers and the contributor's own private status view.
   - Acceptance: Tests reject blank, malformed, duplicate-conflicting, or unauthorized attestations and prove public catalogue and peer surfaces contain none of the private declaration fields.

3. **Private single-media package**: Each submission accepts exactly one original short video and one required still poster fallback, bound to one selected programme, with a private pre-review asset location.
   - Current: Candidate JSON can describe `local-preview`, embed, or illustration media but has no controlled binary upload, poster fallback, or contributor ownership link.
   - Target: The pilot accepts a video no longer than 30 seconds plus its poster fallback as one bounded package; zero or multiple video packages, absent poster fallback, unsupported types, and over-limit duration are rejected before persistence.
   - Acceptance: Contract tests cover zero, one, and two video inputs; a 30-second video is eligible, a longer one is rejected, and no public asset URL is returned before approval and release.

4. **Houston Tillotson draft review**: A Houston Tillotson-associated submission remains a non-endorsed private draft until an independent active staff reviewer passes its evidence review and an authorized publisher includes it in the existing governed release.
   - Current: Staff reviewers can approve catalogue candidates but cannot evaluate contributor consent or create a contributor-owned draft.
   - Target: The pilot has a review state distinct from public publication, shows the school association as a factual selection rather than official endorsement, and requires reviewer approval plus normal governed publishing.
   - Acceptance: A contributor cannot publish, a reviewer cannot publish without the publisher capability, and an approved-but-unreleased draft remains absent from all learner responses.

5. **Contributor revocation**: The contributor can revoke a draft or published submission, causing its public projection to be removed immediately and preventing later release without a new submission and review.
   - Current: Catalogue retirement is administrator-operated and has no contributor consent withdrawal flow.
   - Target: A contributor-owned revocation action is idempotent, wins over an in-flight release, preserves only a minimum private audit event, and removes the public media projection without deleting the provider's factual text and official sources.
   - Acceptance: Tests prove repeated revocation is safe, a release racing with revocation cannot expose the asset, and the provider detail falls back to factual text and sources.

6. **Synthetic visual placeholder safety**: Temporary AI-generated campus visuals are clearly marked as illustrative, non-official context and never represent Houston Tillotson University or another named school as a photograph.
   - Current: The visual-first discovery work is media-free and the catalogue has no approved synthetic-image policy or label.
   - Target: Any temporary generated still is original and carries fixed disclosure text; it contains no Google Maps, Street View, scraped/provider imagery, real-campus likeness, logo, seal, mascot, readable signage, or endorsement claim.
   - Acceptance: UI and content checks show the disclosure alongside each temporary visual and reject an empty label, a school-representation claim, or a non-approved asset classification.

7. **Choice and privacy separation**: Contributor eligibility, rights records, uploads, withdrawals, and visual interactions are never recommendation inputs, provider disclosures, or learner-visible contributor data.
   - Current: Existing catalogue and profile logic does not model contributor media, but some platform features contain public uploader profiles and engagement signals.
   - Target: The pilot stores only the minimum private contributor/review information needed for the workflow and keeps every submission/consent/media event outside ranking, fit explanations, provider payloads, and unapproved analytics.
   - Acceptance: Regression tests show identical discovery order and explanations with or without contributor activity, and public API payloads exclude contributor account IDs, consent details, private asset paths, and review notes.

## Boundaries

**In scope:**
- Invite-only, signed-in adult-student submissions for the first controlled school pilot.
- One original ≤30-second video plus required poster fallback, private rights attestation, staff review, governed release, and contributor revocation.
- Houston Tillotson University as the first selected-school association, held as non-endorsed/private until the review and release gates pass.
- Clearly labelled, original synthetic illustrative stills as a temporary visual layer.
- Automated regression coverage for access, attestation, private/public boundaries, review/release, revocation, visual disclosure, and choice preservation.

**Out of scope:**
- General public upload, self-service institution onboarding, or minor participation — the pilot is invite-only adult access until separate identity, safeguarding, and moderation work is approved.
- Age or identity verification beyond the signed-in invited-account and 18+ attestation boundary — a stronger verification product requires separate data-lifecycle design.
- Auto-publication, peer-to-peer content delivery, contributor messaging, provider endorsements, or a social feed — every asset remains governed catalogue material.
- Google Maps, Street View, provider-site scraping, collected third-party media, logos, or near-replicas of real campuses — they are not approved source material for temporary visuals.
- Recommendation/fit changes, passive behavioural profiling, or new usage-data collection from media activity — these need a separate privacy-approved product-health design.

## Constraints

- Retain Next.js, NextAuth, TypeScript, Vercel, the existing governed catalogue boundary, and incremental whole-document persistence safety.
- Private consent and review evidence must never use `CatalogueRenderableMedia`, public `sourceUrl` fields, learner API payloads, audit reasons, or client-side storage.
- A parent/guardian consent prompt is deferred for a future minor-media design; the present pilot permits self-signing only for an invited adult who affirms 18+.
- The implementation must preserve the Phase 13 factual fallback, limited motion, pause control, and reduced-motion stop requirements.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Only authenticated, staff-invited accounts that affirm 18+ can start a contributor submission; no other account receives a capability or private draft.
- [ ] A submission is rejected unless it includes a nonblank private attestation, signer name, timestamp, creator authority, and recognisable-person consent declaration.
- [ ] The pilot accepts exactly one ≤30-second video and exactly one poster fallback; zero, two, unsupported, missing-poster, and over-limit inputs are rejected.
- [ ] Private asset locations, consent records, signer identifiers, and review notes never appear in learner responses, public snapshot media, browser storage, or audit reasons.
- [ ] Houston Tillotson association is displayed only as a non-endorsed factual selection; draft and approved-but-unreleased submissions are not public.
- [ ] An independent reviewer and authorized publisher are both required before an approved submission can be publicly projected.
- [ ] Revocation is repeat-safe, blocks concurrent release, immediately removes public media, and preserves factual provider text and official source links.
- [ ] Synthetic visuals show a fixed “AI-generated illustration — not an official campus photograph” disclosure and reject prohibited source/identity claims.
- [ ] No contributor, consent, asset, or interaction data can alter discovery order, fit explanations, or provider payloads.
- [ ] **Prohibition:** The pilot must not admit minors, unauthenticated users, or non-invited accounts to submission.
- [ ] **Prohibition:** The pilot must not automatically publish a contributor asset or imply Houston Tillotson endorsement, attendance, placement, or other outcome.
- [ ] **Prohibition:** The pilot must not treat uploads, consent, or media interactions as a ranking, profiling, or provider-disclosure signal.
- [ ] **Prohibition:** Temporary visuals must not be based on Google Maps, Street View, scraped/provider media, or a real-campus near-replica.
