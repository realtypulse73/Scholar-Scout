# Phase 13.2: Staff Media Intake, Permission Review, and Houston Tillotson Pilot - Context

**Gathered:** 2026-09-29
**Status:** Ready for planning

<domain>
## Phase Boundary

Deliver a private, invite-only adult-student contributor pilot for one original short video and poster fallback. A Houston Tillotson University-associated submission is a non-endorsed factual association only: it remains private until an independent staff review and the existing governed catalogue release both pass. The phase adds neither public uploads nor a social/media feed, and it never uses contributor or media activity as student-choice, recommendation, provider-disclosure, or analytics data.
</domain>

<spec_lock>
## Locked Specification

`13.2-SPEC.md` contains the seven locked product requirements and acceptance criteria. The decisions below only choose implementation shape; they must not weaken, duplicate, or expand the locked adult-only, private-by-default, governed-release, revocation, disclosure, and choice/privacy boundaries.
</spec_lock>

<decisions>
## Implementation Decisions

### Adult invitation and self-attestation
- **D-01:** Staff grant a single, bounded contributor invitation to a known signed-in account. The private contributor route must not reveal submission capability, consent data, or asset metadata to signed-out, uninvited, disabled, or withdrawn accounts.
- **D-02:** Before a private draft exists, the invited contributor completes a plain-language, self-signing adult gate: they affirm that they are 18 or older, provide their signer name, and make a dated declaration of creator authority plus consent from every recognisable person. This is an adult self-attestation pilot, not age/identity verification and not the deferred parent/guardian flow for minors.
- **D-03:** The contributor starts with a compact purpose statement: the material is submitted for Scholar Scout's governed school-information workflow, not as an official Houston Tillotson University post, provider endorsement, attendance claim, placement claim, or promise of outcomes.

### Private single-package upload
- **D-04:** After the adult gate, the contributor may create one private package for one selected programme: exactly one original video of at most 30 seconds and one required still poster fallback. The private upload view validates file count, allowed type, duration, poster presence, and all required attestation fields before the package can proceed to review.
- **D-05:** Uploads remain private throughout draft and review. The server owns authorization and returns no learner-facing asset URL, private storage location, signer detail, or review evidence to the browser outside the contributor/reviewer views that require it.
- **D-06:** The contributor experience uses explicit private lifecycle feedback: `Draft`, `Ready for review`, `Action needed`, `Approved for release`, `Published`, and `Removed`. It supports a safe retry after an interrupted or failed upload without creating a second active package; detailed reviewer-only notes stay private while contributor-visible guidance is concise and actionable.

### Independent review, governed release, and withdrawal
- **D-07:** The Houston Tillotson association is a factual selected-school label, presented as non-endorsed. Submission, review approval, and governed publication are separate gates: the contributor cannot publish; a reviewer cannot perform the publisher's release action; and approval alone never exposes media to learners.
- **D-08:** Staff reuse the governed catalogue candidate and snapshot release boundary rather than creating a parallel public media channel. A reviewer validates the private attestation and content package; an authorized publisher subsequently decides whether the reviewed candidate enters the existing release flow.
- **D-09:** The contributor has a visible, repeat-safe `Remove my submission` control from every non-final private status. Removal immediately strips any public media projection, wins over a concurrent release attempt, leaves factual programme text and official sources intact, and requires a new invitation/submission/review for any future content.

### Public presentation and temporary illustration
- **D-10:** If a reviewed package is released, its learner-facing attribution is anonymous: use `Student-contributed perspective` rather than a name, account, profile, school relationship, or personal biography. Keep the quiet non-endorsement statement near the media, with factual source/actions still more important than the contribution.
- **D-11:** Any temporary synthetic still is an original, generic learning-environment illustration only. It must show the fixed disclosure `AI-generated illustration — not an official campus photograph`, never claim to depict Houston Tillotson University or another named school, and never use Google Maps, Street View, scraped/provider material, a real-campus near-replica, logos, seals, mascots, or readable signage as source/reference material.
- **D-12:** The temporary illustration is supporting detail-page context rather than the authoritative provider record or a simulated school photograph. It must remain visually and semantically distinct from the factual provider source, media label, and official verification actions.

### Choice and privacy boundary
- **D-13:** Invitation status, adult attestation, submissions, rights records, reviewer activity, release status, removal, and media interactions stay outside ranking, fit explanations, provider payloads, browser storage, public audit reasons, and new usage analytics. The pilot collects only the minimum private workflow data required to run the submission and review process.

### Preview-only owner demo exception
- **D-14:** The explicitly configured project owner, while signed in with the existing active editor, reviewer, and administrator capabilities, may self-invite, submit, self-approve, and perform the existing governed release of their own test package only in an explicitly enabled Vercel Preview deployment. This narrowly permits students shown that Preview demo to see a clearly labelled anonymous demo projection. The exception is server-derived from the trusted session and exact Preview runtime/owner configuration, is denied for Production, local, missing, malformed, or otherwise ambiguous configuration, and accepts no browser-controlled demo flag. It does not grant upload rights to students, weaken the independent reviewer/publisher path for ordinary contributor media, add ranking, analytics, provider disclosure, or learner-visible private fields, or make Preview demo media deliverable in Production.

### the agent's Discretion
- Choose the safe private-storage implementation, file-type allowlist, byte-size ceilings, duration-inspection mechanism, expiry length for invitations, and encrypted/secret-handling approach after researching the existing Vercel/Next.js deployment constraints.
- Choose exact component layout, validation copy, accessible upload progress/retry presentation, staff review checklist presentation, and the concise wording for `Action needed`, provided they preserve the locked lifecycle, privacy, and non-endorsement boundaries.
- Reuse the existing account/session, active-staff capability, candidate revision, conditional-write, public snapshot, factual fallback, source/action, motion, and accessibility patterns rather than creating an alternate discovery/release path.
</decisions>

<specifics>
## Specific Ideas

- Houston Tillotson University is the first controlled test association because the user expects to begin with original content from an adult daughter who knows the school; the current app must nevertheless show no official HTU affiliation until ordinary review/release controls pass.
- The pilot is intentionally conservative while the app is not broadly promoted: an adult student can submit their own content, but only through a staff invitation and never directly to the public catalogue.
- The desired public feeling is visually inviting yet truthful: a student's approved perspective can add texture, while the governed programme facts, sources, comparison, and official next action remain clear and independent.
- The current temporary-image preview is not a repository asset or approved provider representation. Any eventual use must comply with D-11 and pass the normal Phase 13/13.2 review gates.
</specifics>

<code_context>
## Existing Code Insights

### Reusable Assets
- `catalogue-publication.ts` and `server/catalogue-publications.ts` already model candidate revisions, staff review, a release gate, and a fail-closed public media projection; contributor-private data must be modeled alongside, not inside, the public `CatalogueRenderableMedia` shape.
- `data-store.ts` provides the persisted-document and conditional-write seam needed for invitation, draft, review, release, and revocation transitions, but it does not establish a binary-asset store.
- `student-actor.ts`, `auth.ts`, and active-staff authorization provide separate authenticated-contributor and capability-checked reviewer/publisher boundaries.
- `DiscoveryPreviewSlot.tsx` and the focus/detail components preserve factual fallback and can render only a published projection plus clear labels—not consent evidence or private asset locations.

### Established Patterns
- Learner surfaces read a reviewed catalogue snapshot only; drafts and approved-but-unreleased candidates must stay absent.
- Publication uses revision checks and distinct staff capabilities. Stale actions return conflict rather than silently overwriting a newer decision.
- Client components use native labelled controls, visible focus, server-validated requests, responsive reflow, and user-facing error state. Server-only persistence and private evidence must stay out of browser storage and public JSON.

### Integration Points
- Add a bounded contributor-media domain model and private server routes tied to the existing signed-in account and conditional-write lifecycle.
- Add a distinct staff-review seam for private contributor consent/package evidence, then feed only an approved, revocation-aware public candidate projection into the existing catalogue publication/release path.
- Extend staff UI with the necessary private review controls and add an invited-contributor page/status view; leave discovery ordering, fit logic, provider response DTOs, and public media renderer intentionally insulated.
- Add contract and regression coverage for invitation/attestation, lifecycle/race behavior, privacy projection, governed release, immediate revocation, disclosure text, factual fallback, and unchanged recommendation output.
</code_context>

<deferred>
## Deferred Ideas

- Parent/guardian consent, all minor participation, stronger age/identity verification, safeguarding workflow, and expanded retention policy require a separate minor-media design.
- Public/self-service student uploads, open school onboarding, direct provider submissions, contributor profiles, comments, peer delivery, likes, and a media feed are outside this controlled pilot.
- Any provider clearance/endorsement workflow, third-party content license, Google Maps/Street View/embed review, campus-photograph permission, or use of real-school visual material requires separate rights/terms/privacy/accessibility approval.
- New media engagement analytics, preference-data collection from media activity, recommendation changes, or student/provider data sharing require a separate privacy-approved product-health phase.
- Phase 13.1's media-free visual welcome remains a separately planned presentation-only release; this pilot does not make it an asset-driven first-arrival experience.
</deferred>
