The Phase 13 Preview-only fixture now uses two original, photorealistic generic learning scenes: applied-health practice and welding/fabrication practice. Neither represents a real school, provider, campus, programme offer, or person.

## Truth and replacement boundaries

- The learner-facing disclosure now says `AI-generated image — not an official campus photograph`, matching the photo-real presentation without claiming a real photo.
- Each card remains labeled `Preview test fixture`, has unknown factual fields, and links only to the fixture source context.
- Original V1 illustrations remain alongside the new V2 files, so the replacement is recoverable.
- Later reviewed original, licensed, or provider-approved media will use the same governed media-record path; no data-model migration is required.
- The images are still not video and do not complete the separate pause/reduced-motion video UAT.

## Image-generation prompts

- Applied health: original photorealistic generic skills lab, adult learners, training mannequin, no real campus, logos, text, or identifiable people.
- Welding and fabrication: original photorealistic generic vocational workshop, safe protective equipment, no real campus, logos, text, or identifiable people.
